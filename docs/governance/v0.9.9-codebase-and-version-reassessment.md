# v0.9.9 Codebase and Version Reassessment

Status: Proposal for owner review, snapshot 2026-07-16.

This is an R0 inventory and release recommendation. It changes no profile,
range, CRC/header behavior, processor authority, runtime route, support claim,
version file, branch, or tag.

## Executive conclusion

Do not tag v0.9.9 yet. Use v0.9.9 as the last 0.9 convergence release: retire
the remaining v1 runtime authorities except the Legacy Combiner executable and
constrained runner, close or explicitly de-scope the R3 evidence rows, integrate
the open hardening/parity work, restore required CI, and meet the accepted
54,000-line ceiling.

The planned IC onboarding toolchain is too large a feature to call `0.9.10`.
Under semantic versioning, `0.9.10` is another patch in the 0.9 line. Rename the
next feature milestone to v0.10.0, then use v0.11.0 for UI-driven onboarding and
v0.12.x for internal release candidates and stabilization.

Enter v1.0.0 after two internal v0.12.x validation cycles complete without
changing the supported data/config contracts. Code size alone is not a major-
version trigger; the trigger is a stable compatibility and support promise.

## Measured codebase state

- The canonical accepted v0.9.8 production ratchet is 56,742 nonblank C#/AXAML
  lines. Current v0.9.9 changes do not establish a new accepted ratchet.
- The four remaining compatibility authorities contain 2,141 nonblank C# lines
  before caller cleanup.
- Deleting only those definitions would produce about 54,601 lines, still over
  the owner-approved v0.9.9 ceiling of 54,000.
- Removing their replaced Workbench adapters and duplicated projections gives a
  current forecast of 53,000-53,800 lines, with 53,300 as the working target.
- A focused scan found no production/test/profile `TODO`, `FIXME`, `HACK`, or
  `NotImplementedException`. Remaining work is represented by architecture,
  promotion blockers, evidence gates, and release state rather than comments.

## P0: remaining v1 runtime retirement

| Authority | Size | Current external consumers | Required replacement before deletion |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `CompositionProfileCompiler` | 776 | 2 | Trusted V2 compilation for CtrlRAM Replace and General Replace, including diagnostics, reports, optional-slot behavior, protected ranges, and byte parity |
| `TpFlashMapCatalog` | 497 | 13 | One V2-backed query boundary for map, display, coverage, number policy, postbuild-to-region bindings, and binary-model facts |
| `IcMetadataFacade` | 123 | 5 | The same typed V2 query boundary must replace UI/workbench catalog aggregation and firmware-version selection |
| `LegacyCombinerPostbuildCatalog` | 745 | 4 direct plus transitive consumers | Manifest-pinned V2 processor declarations with exact tool/command/staging/read/write facts and per-case golden/owner review |

The Legacy Combiner executable and its constrained staged runner are explicitly
retained. The runner is about 648 lines across three files and remains the sole
writer for approved Combiner-owned CRC/header mutations. C# must not replace
its algorithms merely to reduce line count.

Retirement order:

1. close NT51926 Common FW 1.4.1 cascade golden/owner evidence;
2. make the trusted V2 route match the selected Workbench operation, diagnostic,
   report, and full-output behavior without promoting support;
3. migrate one evidenced IC/version/count family at a time and delete its exact
   old declarations and callers in the same phase;
4. route map/metadata/display/coverage consumers through one V2 query boundary;
5. move the reviewed static postbuild declarations into V2 processor data while
   retaining the runner; and
6. close General Replace policy and delete the final legacy compiler caller.

## P0: evidence and policy gaps

The versioned evidence paths are in
`testdata/golden/owner-handoff/v0.9.9/README.md`. The highest-risk unresolved
items are:

- NT51926 1.4.1 cascade independent expected output, exact Combiner tool/command
  trace, allowed-difference approval, and firmware-owner authority review;
- selected CtrlRAM IC/version/count expected outputs or exact workflow-scoped
  aliases, including the unresolved NT51931 tool/mode decision;
- CtrlRAM base artifact shape and TP firmware-version edit authority;
- General Replace protected ranges, explicit-mapping envelope,
  overlap/alignment, TP-postbuild trigger, and release-scope matrix;
- NT51919/NT51932 AB direct evidence or fact-scoped AB aliases;
- NT51929/NT51951 AB product goldens and owner review; and
- NT51950 AB runtime-promotion review. Its existing direct goldens and
  Python-vs-Legacy Combiner 1.13 byte parity do not need regeneration.

Nineteen profiles currently record open blockers:

- 18 are `executable-candidate`: 13 General Merge profiles, four AB profiles
  (NT51919/NT51929/NT51932/NT51950), and NT51926 CtrlRAM Replace;
- NT51951 AB is only `compilable`, with direct-golden and firmware-owner blockers;
- all 13 General Merge rows require legacy parity review and a support decision;
  this does not mean all 13 must be promoted; and
- support-neutral rows can remain candidates, but no unsupported row may keep a
  hidden dependency on an obsolete v1 runtime authority.

Golden policy now requires every accepted replay input/output to be committed
under `testdata/golden/` after manifest/hash, provenance, personal-information,
and owner review. Project/product names remain; personal identity does not.

## P0: golden privacy tooling gap

The current `scripts/intake_ic_reference.py` is safe as an ignored quarantine,
but it is not yet a golden-promotion tool:

- it records an absolute `sourceDirectory`, which can contain a personal Windows
  profile path;
- `--owner` and `--source-ref` are free-form and can accidentally contain names,
  email addresses, usernames, or personal transfer links;
- it does not inspect OOXML/PDF/archive metadata for author or last-saved-by
  identities; and
- it does not create a sanitized tracked workflow manifest and replay case.

Before the next golden promotion, add one focused promotion/privacy gate or
equivalent tested validation. It should keep project identifiers, replace human
identity with stable role labels, reject personal paths/URLs, inspect document
container metadata, generate canonical hashes, and never copy an external tool
binary merely because it appeared in evidence intake.

Current fetched branch state:

  in that comparison.
- `origin/0.9.10` is not a linear continuation of `origin/0.9.9`: v0.9.9 has
  three unique commits and v0.9.10 has two unique commits.
- `VERSION` still reads `0.9.8`.
- PRs #132, #133, #134, and #138 are all open drafts targeting `0.9.9`; their
  current check rollups are failed. The inspected failures are GitHub billing/
  runner allocation failures (`runner_id=0`, no executed steps), not local test
  evidence, but required CI is still not green.

Consequences:

- resolve/integrate or explicitly close every open v0.9.9 PR before the version
  branch final review;
- do not base further release work on the divergent `origin/0.9.10` history;
  carry its verified feature commits onto the owner-selected v0.10.0 branch
  only after v0.9.9 converges;
- update version/release files only in the final release phase; and
- do not tag while required CI, owner evidence, code-size, package, or review
  gates remain open.

## P2: optimization candidates after authority retirement

Large files or aggregates are not automatically waste. The first deletion
target is duplicate firmware semantics, not safety validation or arbitrary file
splitting.

- `WorkbenchCompositionService` spans about 4,483 nonblank lines across 41
  partial files. Delete only the adapters/projections made obsolete by the V2
  query and execution routes.
- `MainWindowViewModel` spans about 2,847 lines across 22 partial files. It is a
  UI maintainability candidate after runtime convergence, not a v0.9.9 firmware
  parity shortcut.
- `CompiledComposition` and the V2 compiler/lowering code are large because they
  enforce bounds, authority, deterministic order, and validation. Do not remove
  checks without proof that another single canonical gate already enforces the
  same invariant.
- Hex editor and large AXAML/style files can be simplified later, but they do
  not remove the duplicated v1 firmware authority and therefore are lower
  priority than the four retirement surfaces.

Ponytail deletion review:

- `CompositionProfileCompiler*.cs` — delete 776 legacy-authority lines after the two remaining workflows have V2 parity; replace with the trusted V2 compiler.
- `TpFlashMapCatalog*.cs` plus `IcMetadataFacade.cs` — delete 620 duplicated catalog/facade lines after all callers use one V2 query boundary.
- `LegacyCombinerPostbuildCatalog*.cs` — move/delete 745 static declaration lines after exact evidence is expressed in V2 processor data; retain the 648-line runner.
- Workbench callers/projections — delete the adapter glue made redundant by those three migrations; do not preserve compatibility wrappers with no external consumer.

Net: approximately 2,900-3,700 production lines can be removed without reducing
features or validation, yielding the forecast 53,000-53,800 range. The estimate
is not a release ratchet until the actual diff and canonical counter pass.

## Proposed milestone and tag sequence

### v0.9.9 — final 0.9 convergence release

Tag only when:

- no production runtime depends on the four obsolete v1 authorities;
- Legacy Combiner executable/runner remains constrained and tested;
- every release-scoped route has a tracked, privacy-reviewed golden and required
  firmware-owner approval, while de-scoped routes are explicitly unsupported;
- canonical production code is at or below 54,000 lines;
- PR scope, reviews, local full verification, required CI, clean Windows package,
  hashes/SBOM/provenance, and release gates are green; and
- `VERSION`, documentation, package metadata, branch head, and tag agree.

### v0.10.0 — config-driven IC onboarding toolchain

- import owner-defined XLSX/sheet data, mmap headers, BAT/command references,
  sample inputs, and expected outputs into deterministic candidate records;
- generate/validate manifests, hashes, ranges, slots, processor declarations,
  and replay tests without manually editing many C# intermediate layers;
- remain feature-freeze/support-neutral: generation never promotes an IC;
- require privacy/golden and firmware-owner gates before tracked promotion; and
- start from the converged v0.9.9 history, not the divergent 0.9.10 branch.

### v0.11.0 — UI authoring and validation

- expose the stable onboarding contract through UI forms/import;
- preview derived IC facts, errors, diffs, and generated candidate changes;
- export a reviewable pack/change set rather than compiling arbitrary user code;
- establish signed pack/version/update manifest contracts; and
- keep runtime support promotion separate from UI data entry.

### v0.12.0-rc.1 and v0.12.x — product stabilization

- freeze the onboarding/profile/processor manifest contracts;
- lock the selected IC/workflow support matrix and golden coverage;
- run internal users through clean-machine, signed package, update-channel,
  rollback, SBOM, provenance, and long-run validation;
- allow only defect fixes and evidence closure; and
- complete at least two internal validation cycles on unchanged contracts, for
  example v0.12.0 and v0.12.1.

### v1.0.0 — first major compatibility promise

Enter v1.0.0 immediately after the v0.12.x gates above pass. v1.0 means:

- normal users add supported IC definitions through config/toolchain rather than
  direct compilation or manual C# intermediate layers;
- all advertised routes are owner-approved and golden-reproducible from a fresh
  clone;
- no obsolete v1 runtime remains except the explicitly retained Legacy Combiner
  executable/runner boundary;
- package/signing/update contracts and backward-compatibility policy are frozen;
  and
- release CI and clean-machine validation are repeatable.

The full automatic updater UI may ship in v1.1 if v1.0 already freezes the
signed release/update manifest and compatibility contract. Replacing Legacy
Combiner itself, or making a breaking profile/pack/onboarding schema change
after v1, should be evaluated as a v2.0 boundary.
