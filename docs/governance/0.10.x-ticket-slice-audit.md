# 0.10.x Ticket Execution-Slice Audit

Status: Active admission audit, 2026-07-25.

This audit applies the ticket-execution admission policy to every owner-approved
`0.10.x` ticket in `#170`–`#197`, plus the deferred Presentation epics `#207`
and `#208`. It does not change an issue's product outcome, risk class,
evidence requirement, or `Blocked by` edge. `Split` means the parent issue must
gain independently admitted child tickets when slices have different blockers,
evidence gates, or merge timing; same-blocker slices may instead use explicitly
recorded ordered phases. `Phase` means ordered phase commits are required but a
new GitHub issue is not inherently necessary because their blockers and merge
boundary are shared. `Keep` means the issue is a single bounded vertical
outcome after its stated dependencies are satisfied.

`ACn` refers to acceptance checkbox *n* in the linked live GitHub issue. The
short source phrase is a locator, not a replacement for the issue body.

## Immediate ordering correction

The dependency graph remains valid, but it is not sufficient for parallel
implementation. The first frontier exposed an unrecorded mutable-surface
collision:

- #172 owns `scripts/verify.py` and
  `tests/scripts/test_verify_orchestration.py` for its active verifier slice.
- #171 waits for integrated #172, then becomes the sole owner of coverage
  policy, baselines, collectors, verifier/CI integration, and evidence.
- #170 is fully headless and independent of verifier ownership. Its deferred
  Settings consumer is #207 and does not belong to `0.10.1`.

No current `#170`–`#172` feature branch is integration-ready merely because an
earlier local or CI result passed. Each must be re-admitted against this audit
and its exact latest review findings.
