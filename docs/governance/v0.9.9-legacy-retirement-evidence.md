# v0.9.9 Legacy Retirement Evidence

## Decision

At baseline `c3b77363`, no remaining legacy authority can be deleted safely as
a whole. The remaining duplication is architectural rather than byte-identical:
trusted V2 profiles own the migrated Standard Merge and DP Replace paths, while
CtrlRAM and General Replace still depend on Workbench-built profiles, flash-map
projections, and metadata aggregation. The static Combiner Postbuild catalog has
now been retired into a hash-pinned repository data catalog.

The original inventory was R0. ADR 0024's TP/full-Flash convergence is R3
because it adds a schema/compiler processor-prefix contract and a second exact
map capacity. It does not change a command, write authority, runtime route, or
support claim.

## Measured retirement surface

The accepted `v0.9.8` production ratchet is 56,742 nonblank C#/AXAML lines.
The v0.9.9 TP semantic/Workbench/UI convergence, bilingual-resource
consolidation, drop-zone convergence, CLI report-writer convergence, and
firmware-region ordering convergence and Postbuild data-authority phases lower the interim ratchet to
55,260 lines, keep the
Workbench aggregate at 4,477, and lower the MainWindowViewModel aggregate to
2,717. They retire only unbound projections, wrappers, and duplicated
bilingual assignment and region-ordering structures plus the static Postbuild
command declarations; they do not promote any IC/mode. The remaining gap to the owner-approved
54,000-line stable gate is 1,260 lines.
The three remaining compatibility authorities account for 1,362 nonblank C#
lines before caller cleanup:

| Authority | Files | Nonblank lines | External production caller files |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `CompositionProfileCompiler` | 8 | 776 | 2 |
| `TpFlashMapCatalog` | 4 | 507 | 9 |
| `IcMetadataFacade` | 1 | 79 | 6 |

Deleting those definitions without migrating their callers would not compile.
Their nominal subtraction would leave 53,898 lines, so reaching the owner-
approved `v0.9.9` ceiling of 54,000 requires the evidence-backed caller
migrations and authority deletion to land together. It must not come from
deleting evidence or weakening validation. This is a measured boundary, not a
deletion claim or runtime-promotion approval.

## Current consumers and exact replacement conditions

| Current authority | Current production responsibility | V2 replacement required before deletion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CompositionProfileCompiler` | Lowers the synthetic CtrlRAM Replace profile and General Replace explicit mappings built by `WorkbenchCompositionService` | Trusted V2 registrations must express both workflows, preserve optional CtrlRAM slot behavior and General Replace safety policy, and produce equivalent operations, diagnostics, reports, and bytes |
| `TpFlashMapCatalog` | Supplies CtrlRAM/General Replace region planning, display, coverage, IC-number choices, and postbuild-to-region binding | Resolved V2 family/map/profile facts must supply every consumer without copying ranges, aliases, number policy, or postbuild mapping into another C# catalog |
| `IcMetadataFacade` | Joins IC support, flash maps, number choices, and postbuild profiles for Workbench/UI catalog and firmware-metadata selection | One typed V2-backed query boundary must expose the same catalog, number-selection, version-based postbuild choice, and readiness information |

The Legacy Combiner 1.13.0 executable and constrained staged runner are not
retirement targets. The duplicated static declarations have moved to
`profiles/built-in/ctrlram-postbuild-v2/catalog.json`; the loader rejects hash,
unknown-field, enum, range, duplicate-id, and reviewed argument-allowlist drift.
The runner remains the sole writer for approved Combiner-owned CRC/header changes.

## Existing V2 coverage

The only built-in CtrlRAM Replace V2 candidate is
`nt51926-ctrlram-replace-fw141-cascade`. It is restricted to NT51926,
Common FW 1.4.1 and cascade selection. It accepts exactly `0x3C000` TP-work or
`0x40000` full-Flash base forms. Both select the same five required
Normal/DIFF/MP/VN/NF inputs and the pinned Combiner 1.13 invocation profile. For
full Flash, the shared engine stages only `[0,0x3C000)` and preserves
`[0x3C000,0x40000)` byte-for-byte.

This is a compiler/executor contract and synthetic tail-preservation proof,
not direct full-Flash product golden evidence. No runtime promotion is claimed.

It remains `executable-candidate` and deliberately has no UI/CLI runtime route.
Its profile records three open blockers: direct input/output golden evidence,
firmware-owner review of the exact command and writes, and runtime-route review.
The current Workbench path is not semantically equivalent because it also:

- permits a non-empty subset of the mapped CtrlRAM regions;
- catalogs, routes, and plans all 13 IC rows and their single/cascade/numeric
  branches without making a support claim;
- selects versioned NT51926 and NT51930 postbuild categories from the base;
- exposes a Build-only TP firmware-version edit that remains R3-gated on its
  existing field/range, expected-output, and firmware-owner evidence;
- plans display, coverage, warnings, command evidence, reports, and naming; and
- admits General Replace mappings only through its current protected-range and
  postbuild selection policy.

The committed NT51926 and NT51927 self-replacement fixtures prove that the
Workbench route and staged tool can execute. For NT51926 Common FW 1.4.1
cascade, regression commit `6eb0db6e` also runs the current Workbench compiler
and the V2 candidate from the same owner-approved inputs through the actual
Combiner 1.13 staged runner and requires their complete output bytes and
SHA-256 to match. This is cross-route migration parity, not an independently
owner-approved expected output. The fixtures intentionally omit expected
postbuild outputs, so the direct golden and firmware-owner gates remain open.

## Retirement order

1. Close NT51926 Common FW 1.4.1 cascade TP/full-Flash golden and owner review
   against the ADR 0024 zero-based prefix contract. Do not promote runtime
   support as part of evidence intake.
2. Migrate one directly evidenced IC/version/count family at a time. In the same
   phase, replace its flash-map, number-policy, and postbuild projections and
   delete the exactly replaced C# declarations.
3. Route all CtrlRAM planning, display, coverage, metadata, Preview/Build, and
   report consumers through the reviewed V2 boundary. Retire
   `IcMetadataFacade` and the covered parts of `TpFlashMapCatalog` only after no
   production consumer remains.
4. Obtain owner approval for General Replace protected ranges, the explicit-
   mapping envelope, overlap/alignment policy, TP-touching expected outputs,
   and release-scope IC/modes. Express only those reviewed safety envelopes and
   postbuild triggers in trusted V2 data, prove operation/report/byte parity,
   and remove the remaining `CompositionProfileCompiler` caller.
5. The static `LegacyCombinerPostbuildCatalog` is retired; keep its hash-pinned
   data projection under parity tests. Remove `CompositionProfileCompiler` and
   `TpFlashMapCatalog` only when architecture tests prove they have no
   production consumers. Keep the Legacy Combiner executable, constrained
   runner, staging-copy isolation, host diff enforcement, and tool packaging.

## Protected invariants

- No range, command, processor write, alias, IC-count rule, or runtime support
  is inferred from this inventory.
- For zero-offset CtrlRAM sources, the declared section length is an upper
  bound: copy stops at source EOF, preserves the untouched base tail, and
  rejects a source larger than the declared maximum. This owner-approved
  compatibility behavior is not whole-input exact-length authority.
- C# does not reimplement Combiner CRC/header algorithms. In particular, C#
  never writes the AB header CRC.
- NT51950/NT51951 AB use the full DP container as the base and do not require
  `map.txt`; Python parity is compared with the exact Legacy Combiner 1.13
  command.
- NT51919/NT51932 AB promotion still requires direct evidence or the explicitly
  approved fact-scoped alias evidence documented for that workflow.
- Runtime/support promotion remains separate and requires owner review after
  byte evidence closes.
- The NT51926 CtrlRAM V2 candidate accepts only exact TP-work/full-Flash shapes;
  its prefix/tail tests prove the executor boundary but do not replace direct
  product golden parity.
