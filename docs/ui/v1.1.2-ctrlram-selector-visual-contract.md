# v1.1.2 CtrlRAM selector visual contract

Status: proposed reference for product-owner approval.

Target: `v1.1.2`; this proposed contract and any eventual implementation remain
pending product-owner approval and are not `v1.1.1` release blockers.

Canonical proposed annotated geometry board:
[`v1.1.2-ctrlram-selector-reference.svg`](references/v1.1.2-ctrlram-selector-reference.svg)

The SVG is an annotated design board, not a production viewport render or
pixel oracle. Its numeric annotations define the proposed contract. Exact
reference-versus-actual comparison must use production renders captured at
`980 x 640` and `1440 x 900` logical pixels.

## Source observation

The product owner supplied four Light-theme English screenshots on
2026-09-01. They show the CtrlRAM Replace input surface in constrained and
wider states. The screenshots themselves came from transient clipboard paths,
so this checked-in SVG is the proposed durable reference rather than a copy of
those temporary files.

The observed defects are:

1. selector title, metadata, and selected-state rows become vertically crowded
   as the viewport narrows;
2. Base firmware and the Cascade/Common group surfaces do not share the same
   left and right anchors; and
3. the expanded group can display three visually competing outlines, while
   hover changes which outline is emphasized; and
4. when a file/type title or filename wraps to two lines, the wrapped text,
   `Verified` or other badges, and the following filename/status row are packed
   too tightly.

## Approved direction once the owner accepts the reference

- Base firmware, Cascade, Common, and every equivalent primary selector
  surface share the same horizontal start and end anchors.
- An expanded group has exactly one persistent section outline. Each selected
  input remains one inset card with its own outline. There is no intermediate
  bordered content wrapper.
- Hover never adds, removes, thickens, or moves an outline. It may change only
  the section-header fill, chevron/accent color, and existing focus/hover token.
  Keyboard focus retains a separate accessible focus indicator.
- The narrow-viewport change is vertical spacing only. It does not re-order
  facts, change columns, abbreviate content, alter controls, or change any
  Application/profile/firmware semantics.
- File/type titles and filenames remain content-sized flow items rather than
  fixed one-line slots. When either wraps, its complete line box is reserved;
  the badge row starts `8–12 px` after the wrapped text, and the next
  filename/status row starts `12–16 px` after the badge row. A badge never
  overlays, touches, or vertically centers against only the first line of a
  wrapped label.
- Use a stable vertical rhythm: title to metadata `6–8 px`, metadata to
  selected-state text `10–12 px`, section-header lines `4–6 px`, card vertical
  padding at least `16 px`, and at least `12 px` between primary surfaces.

## Red-capable acceptance evidence

- Render the complete CtrlRAM Replace surface at both reference viewports; do
  not validate cropped controls alone.
- Assert the Base firmware, Cascade, and Common outer left/right bounds match
  within `0.5` logical pixel.
- Assert each expanded group has one section outline and one outline per input
  card, with no intermediate bordered wrapper.
- Compare normal and hovered geometry: all bounds, border thicknesses, and
  content positions remain identical.
- Assert title/metadata/status vertical gaps meet the ranges above at both
  viewports in English and `zh-TW`.
- Force both a two-line file/type title and a two-line filename; assert the
  badge row begins `8–12 px` after the wrapped text and the following
  filename/status begins `12–16 px` after the badge row, with no overlap or
  clipping.
- Treat the SVG positions as illustrative only. Geometry evidence comes from
  the exact production viewport renders and measured bounds above.
- Cover keyboard focus, screen-reader naming, Light, Dark, High Contrast,
  100%, and 125%. Token searches or XAML string checks alone are insufficient.

The eventual production change must reuse the shared selector/group/card
owners. It must not add a CtrlRAM-specific duplicate control or infer selector,
region, topology, processor, or firmware facts in Presentation.
