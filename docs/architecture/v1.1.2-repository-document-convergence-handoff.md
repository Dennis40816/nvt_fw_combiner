# v1.1.2 repository document convergence handoff

Status: documentation-convergence planning is active; future candidate actions
remain pending exact owner approval and their applicable review gates.

## Boundary

`v1.1.2` owns in-place documentation topology, retention, and cleanup
planning. It changes no firmware facts, profiles, ranges, Golden bytes,
packages, releases, or runtime behavior. The root [README](../../README.md)
is the human entry point, the [NFC roadmap](nfc_roadmap.md) is the sole owner
of version allocation and TODO scheduling, and the
[v1.1.2 manifest](v1.1.2-repository-document-convergence-manifest.md) is the
historical deletion-evidence record.

Do not create broad archive trees or a second inventory, TODO, or release
schedule. A deletion, move, or regeneration needs an exact approved path,
caller/link migration when applicable, and proportional validation. A retained
historical document may be marked `Historical`, but age alone is not deletion
authority.

## Protected evidence

Numbered ADRs, canonical Golden evidence, release/package documentation, and
governance records stay in their current canonical locations. They must not be
summarized, relocated, or silently removed by documentation cleanup. The
[manifest](v1.1.2-repository-document-convergence-manifest.md) retains the
frozen D1-D7 identities and links the governed removal to its immutable change
record.

## Approved UI-handoff boundary

The approved UI handoffs link their allocation and completion status to the
canonical [NFC roadmap](nfc_roadmap.md). They retain their unique scope,
diagnosis, exclusions, and evidence, including an explicit no-implementation
and no-firmware-authority boundary. This is documentation routing only; it
does not approve a product, firmware, profile, Golden, or release mutation.

## Current planning control

Current owner-handoff and boilerplate candidates remain pending until their
