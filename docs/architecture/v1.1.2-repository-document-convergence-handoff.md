## Accepted planning decision: in-place convergence

The owner accepted an in-place document convergence strategy. `README.md`
remains the single human entry point, and the existing `architecture`, `adr`,
`contracts`, `governance`, `ui`, `ci`, and `references` topology is preserved.
Do not create broad `active`/`archive`/`history` trees or mass-move documents.
Prioritize navigation, status, content consolidation, and deletion proposals;
make an individual move only when it has concrete value and its link, test, and
package migration is bounded and auditable.

This is a planning decision only. It does not mark repository-wide `v1.1.2`
complete and does not authorize any deletion. Deletions and any future moves
still require their own path-specific evidence and owner approval under the
manifest gate below.

## Accepted planning decision: hybrid status model

The owner accepted a batch-scoped, evidence-backed status model. Do not add a
status field to every document or create a new full central inventory. When a
batch is reviewed, an ambiguously retained historical document gets a
`Historical` marker; generated documents identify their source; and delete
candidates live only in the existing convergence manifest or batch record.
Current authority continues to be determined by existing canonical
owners/locations and existing indexes. `RETAIN_BY_RULE` is terminal current
classification under the approved matcher, not permanent immutability or
deletion authority. These labels describe reviewed evidence and do not
authorize editing all documents.

## Accepted planning decision: risk-lane batching

The owner accepted these bounded lanes for future batches:

- **Lane A** — ungoverned empty or boilerplate READMEs, duplicate TODOs, and
  stale review documents; normally one implementation commit with proportional
  checks.
- **Lane B** — path-sensitive documents referenced by links, tests, packages,
  or provenance; disposition requires bounded caller migration and validation.
- **Lane C** — ADRs, contracts, governance, release, or golden evidence;
  default to retain, and modify, archive, or delete only with separate exact
  owner approval and matching governance/evidence review.

Every Lane A/B/C batch has at most 15 exact paths. When a batch mixes lanes,
the higher lane controls. This policy does not authorize any new deletion.

## Accepted planning decision: version authority

[`docs/architecture/nfc_roadmap.md`](nfc_roadmap.md) is the sole owner of
version allocation and completion status. Work handoffs own scope, acceptance,
evidence, blockers, and the next executable boundary, but not competing release
schedules. Reviews, ADRs, and contracts must not maintain independent version
TODO lists; they link to the roadmap or relevant handoff instead. Duplicate
TODO content is consolidated into its canonical owner and then proposed for
removal under the proper risk lane.

There is one roadmap TODO owner: the canonical roadmap. Handoffs retain only
their unique scope, owner, acceptance, exclusion, diagnosis, and evidence
details.

This defines authority only; it does not itself move or delete content.

## Accepted planning decision: disposition rule

When reviewed, a document with unique decision, Golden, release, license,
audit, or test traceability remains in its existing location and receives a
`Historical` marker where appropriate. A document with no unique content, no
effective inbound reference, and no legal or evidence obligation may be
proposed for exact owner-approved deletion. Do not create or move documents
into an archive tree unless a concrete need for a browsable historical
collection is separately approved with bounded migration. Age alone is not a
disposition reason.

This rule authorizes neither deletion nor relocation; each remains subject to
the applicable risk lane and evidence gate.

## Accepted planning decision: completion model

Repository-wide classification is established by rule coverage plus exact
exception review, not by a manual row for every tracked document path. Every
document must be covered by an approved protected/group rule or an exact-path
disposition; non-exception paths are `RETAIN_BY_RULE`; there must be one version/TODO authority; links, tests, package,
and provenance references must be intact; every deletion or move must have
exact-path evidence; and the root README must reach all current authority.
Unresolved exceptions remain explicitly open, so `v1.1.2` cannot be declared
complete. This model does not create a separate inventory.

## Accepted planning decision: content convergence

For each overlapping set, select one canonical owner and merge only still-valid
unique content into it. Convert remaining projections to short links or exact
deletion candidates after evidence; do not retain parallel full old/new copies.
Immutable ADR, Golden, release, and governance evidence must not be summarized
or merged in a way that changes or destroys the original evidence. Complete
link and caller migration before removing any projection.
