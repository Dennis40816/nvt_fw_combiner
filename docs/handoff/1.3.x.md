## License bridge

The bridge follows decisions 326 and 327.

1. Step 1 ships in `1.3.0` under MIT. Installed updaters accept a manifest that declares MIT or
   `LicenseRef-Proprietary`. Pull request #574 carries it.
2. Step 2 ships in `1.3.1`. `LICENSE` becomes the proprietary text. The manifest declares
   `LicenseRef-Proprietary`.
3. No waiting period separates `1.3.0` and `1.3.1`.
4. A user who never installed `1.3.0` must update to `1.3.0` first.
   The `1.3.1` release notes must say so.
   A Launcher prompt must say so, for example "Update to 1.3.0 first".
5. A person who received a version under MIT keeps MIT for that version.
