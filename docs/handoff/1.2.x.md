## Identity

- **Purpose**: this board records owner decisions for the `1.2.x` trunk and
  its feature branches, the same role [`1.1.12.md`](1.1.12.md) plays for
  `1.1.x`. It does not own product behavior (`SPEC.md`, ADRs, contracts),
  version allocation ([roadmap](../architecture/nfc_roadmap.md)) or completed
  history (`CHANGELOG.md`).
- **Relation to the `1.1.x` board**: decisions 188 and 190 are recorded here.
  Decisions 189 and 191-211 were recorded on [`1.1.12.md`](1.1.12.md) while
  `1.2.x` did not yet carry the `1.1.x` history; the ones that concern `1.2.x`
  are indexed [below](#decisions-on-the-11x-board-that-concern-12x).
  `1.1.x` retired after `v1.2.0` (decisions 200 and 215), so every decision
  from 212 on is recorded here. Splitting the board is a routine commander
  choice, not itself an owner decision, and does not move or restate any
  already-recorded `1.1.x` decision.
- **Decision numbering**: one global sequence across both boards. This board
  continues the sequence at 188; a number is never reused or renumbered when
  `main`, carrying the `1.1.x` line, merges into `1.2.x` (decision 200). From
  decision 212 on, a decision made for `1.2.x` is recorded here even if the
  discussion started on the `1.1.x` board.
- **Trunk `1.2.x`** (decision 184, recorded on [`1.1.12.md`](1.1.12.md#owner-decisions-2026-09-25)):
  cut from `1.1.x` after the `v1.1.14` back-merge (#484) merged, at
  `09d2a6ca2`. `1.2.0` was released from `1.1.x` through the release branch
  `1.2.0` (decision 206) on 2026-09-30 ([closure](#released-v120--2026-09-30)).
  Afterwards `main` merged into `1.2.x` and `1.1.x` retired (decision 200):
  it is frozen, takes no pull requests and is not deleted until an
  owner-approved cleanup list (decision 215). `1.2.x` is the only development
  trunk. The trunk ruleset pattern `*.*.x` covers it.
- Worktrees live in one sibling folder, written `<worktrees>/<name>` in these
  logs, the same convention as the `1.1.x` board.
- Allocation owners: the [1.2.x allocation](1.1.14/1.2.x-allocation.md)
  (decision 175) and the [1.2.x inventory](1.1.14/1.2.x-inventory.md); the
  roadmap [version table](../architecture/nfc_roadmap.md) carries the
  per-version summary. The [status ledger of 2026-10-05](1.2.x-status-2026-10-05.md)
  records where each item really stands. The `1.2.1` wave-1 evaluation reports are in
  [`1.2.1/`](1.2.1/), with their evaluators, reviewers and verdicts in its
  [README](1.2.1/README.md).
