## Limitations

- **No new local timings, build results, or test results.** The 650 s, 14 minutes, and E2 155 s are historical records; CI job medians span multiple sources, while bootstrap TRX contains only one exact-source first run.
- **No UI/core TRX, current local per-part times, or resource peaks.** Therefore no final grouping list, credible gain interval, or commitment to enable U4/U5 is given. A′/B seconds are models under explicit assumptions.

Retry recovery status comes from the manifest; its full admission was not revalidated. This cannot establish passing coverage, overall CI, or release gates.

- Only read-only checks, in-memory recalculation, and report revision were completed in this evaluation; no repository, evidence, or bug-ledger modifications, no verifier/build/tests, and no access to GitHub/network, credentials, DPAPI, or firmware bytes.
