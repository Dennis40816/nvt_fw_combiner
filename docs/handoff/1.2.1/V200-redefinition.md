## Conclusion

**Recommend defining 2.0.0 now as the "general release of the current product contract and evidence baseline" (candidate B)**: replace the "full Launcher general release" theme with traceable current support/evidence, cross-version differences and upgrade/downgrade contracts. Propose explicitly withdrawing the blanket commitment that "all 64 historical routes complete v0.9.16 terminal certification", replacing it with per-route evidence and gap disposition, required Golden cases, rolling comparison for each release, and firmware/release owner approvals bound to the exact candidate. This is **a change to the certification commitment, with no claim of equivalence to the original terminal proof**; it neither invents breaking features for a major version nor promotes `ContractOnly`. Choosing B sets the direction of RO-1 retirement/replacement and **does not amend decision 64**; its required v0.9.16 milestone remains a prerequisite for the formal disposition record. Positioning, replacement content and acceptance conditions can be chosen now, but the current 2.x terminal block remains effective until that evidence, formal rule changes and replacement-gate validation are complete. 〔E01, E03, E11〕
