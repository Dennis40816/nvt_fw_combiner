## 1. Conclusion

**The repository can now provide an inventory of Header/Header-copy geometry, processing paths, and the root cause of the 32-byte difference; it cannot yet complete acceptance of R31-01 firmware-owner facts.** O21 explicitly lists the reader as unknown, and decision 161 also retains this as a prerequisite for any safety claim. This assessment obtained no new firmware-owner evidence to fill the gap (`docs/handoff/1.1.14/1.2.x-inventory.md:502`; `docs/handoff/1.1.12.md:981`).

The following can be stated accurately: the owner judged that **the investigated NT51950 AB Header/Header-copy CRC difference does not affect firmware operation**. This cannot be extended to safety for every IC, topology, bank, boot fallback, or recovery path. A valid CRC recalculation also does not establish that Header copy is unused (`docs/handoff/1.1.12.md:982`; `docs/handoff/1.1.13.md:110`).

The current B finalizer for Partial-family AB CtrlRAM Replace only relocates **ILM/DLM address** and recalculates **Header CRC**; ILM/DLM CRC is outside its write authority and retains the local postbuild result. This distinction directly affects R08's comparison of stage responsibilities and CRC behavior (`src/NvtFwCombiner.Profiles/V2/V2CompositionPlanCompiler.RuntimeReferenceReplace.Banks.cs:386`, `:387`, `:388`).

`1.2.5` should follow the established order: use this report and owner facts to support R08-01/02, then decide the R31-02 reset policy and R31-03 allowance separately. Firmware facts, reset policy, and Golden allowance are three independent decisions; the inventory does not preauthorize a fix (`docs/handoff/1.1.14/1.2.x-allocation.md:172`; `docs/handoff/1.1.14/1.2.x-inventory.md:457`).

## Questions for the owner to decide

The project owner is asked to answer the following six questions **in the capacity of firmware owner**: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q3b, Q4, and Q5. The owner can reply with the options directly, without reading NFC code; please identify the applicable IC, IC count, and firmware version, and leave unknown parts as `unknown`. In multiple-choice questions, “none read/none check” must not be selected together with other substantive options.

Below, “copy” means `FLASHMAP_HEADER_COPY`; “one postbuild pass” means one Combiner processing pass while producing TP; and “B address normalization” means converting B addresses back to addresses relative to that bank's start before running local postbuild. These questions seek the firmware contract; they do not presume that reset or refresh has been approved.
