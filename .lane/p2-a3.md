## Results

- Implemented `rolling --baseline-report FILE` / `run_rolling(baseline_report=...)`.
  The rebuild's eight directly comparable identity members are compared with
  `candidate.executor` in the baseline release's own predecessor report:
  `authorityTrees` (Git subtree ids), `cliSha256` (built CLI hash), `commit`
  (built HEAD), `compilerHost` (embedded PDB runtime/compiler/count),
  `lockFileSetSha256` (original Git-blob locks' JCS inventory hash),
  `resolvedSdkVersion` (dotnet --version under global.json),
  `runtimeClosureSha256` (captured closure inventory JCS hash), `tree`

  field, `tagObject`, is required but admits null (pre-tag candidate) or the
  rebuilt baseline's exact tag object; candidate.version must match the tag.
